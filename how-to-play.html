🎮 How to Play

Welcome to Mini Battle Arena! Choose a game, follow its instructions, and compete with your opponent.

🧠 Memory Match

Match the hidden cards and try to remember their positions. The player with the better score wins.

🎯 Target Hit

Hit the moving target as many times as possible. Take your turns and try to get the highest score.

💥 Dodge Arena

Move your player and avoid the falling blocks. Stay alive as long as possible to beat your opponent.

🎨 Guess Color

A color challenge will appear. Guess the correct color to earn points.

💡 Tips

  • Read the instructions before starting each game.
  • Take turns when the game requires two players.
  • Try different games and compare your scores.
  • Have fun and play fairly!
